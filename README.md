## Breaking Shazam: An Introduction to Audio Fingerprinting

An interactive introduction to **Music Information Retrieval (MIR)** through audio fingerprinting. Inspired by the original Shazam algorithm proposed by [Wang (2003)](https://www.ee.columbia.edu/~dpwe/papers/Wang03-shazam.pdf), this Summer Code explores how computers identify music recordings from short audio excerpts, how audio fingerprints are constructed, and under which conditions the algorithm can fail.

The activities combine theoretical presentations, hands-on implementations, and interactive experiments, allowing participants to understand, reproduce, and challenge a classic music identification system.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this Summer Code, participants should be able to:

- Understand the fundamentals of Music Information Retrieval and audio-based music identification.
- Explain the main concepts behind audio fingerprinting.
- Understand how Shazam constructs and matches audio fingerprints.
- Implement a simplified audio fingerprinting system using Python.
- Investigate the robustness and limitations of audio fingerprinting under different audio transformations.

---

## 🔬 Topics Covered

- Music Information Retrieval (MIR)
- Audio representations and spectrograms
- Spectral peak detection
- Constellation maps
- Audio fingerprint construction
- Hash-based music identification
- Audio matching and temporal alignment
- Noise, pitch shifting, and time stretching
